Project vision:
The project aims to enable privacy-preserving multi-institutional medical imaging by exchanging lightweight generative experts instead of raw data. Institutions contribute independently trained models, and composition is performed post hoc at inference time.

Thesis positioning (updated):
This thesis is now centered on decision-grade evaluation of metadata-driven composition under controlled protocols, followed by a utility-aligned compatibility learning direction.

Current evidence baseline (already achieved):
1. Cross-dataset replication completed on BreakHis and Camelyon17.
2. Locked protocol applied consistently (E1 utility, E2 ranking, E3 system gap).
3. Naive domain-ID conditioning fails all gates on both datasets.
4. Directional pattern is negative (utility and system tendency worsens).
5. Oracle-gap-aware interpretation included to avoid overclaiming.

Core thesis claim:
Naive metadata conditioning (domain-ID concatenation in legacy routed CVAE experts) does not improve compatibility-aligned utility under a locked protocol, even across distinct domain-shift regimes.

What this thesis does and does not claim:
1. Claims supported:
 - This specific conditioning mechanism fails to improve expert utility and routed system performance.
 - Proxy ranking movement does not reliably translate into utility gains.
 - The bottleneck is compatibility estimation aligned to utility, not metadata exposure alone.
2. Claims not supported:
 - Metadata is useless in general.
 - Conditioning can never help.
 - Routing/model composition is fundamentally wrong.

Research questions (finalized):
RQ1. Under a locked protocol, does naive metadata conditioning improve best-expert utility (E1)?
RQ2. Does ranking improvement (E2) translate into system-level gap reduction (E3)?
RQ3. Are failures dataset-specific or consistent across domain-shift regimes?
RQ4. If naive conditioning fails, does a utility-aligned compatibility estimator become the right next modeling direction?

Methodological contract (locked):
1. Seeds: 11, 42, 73.
2. Gates:
 - E1: median relative utility delta <= -0.03 and at least 2 passing seeds.
 - E2: median top1 uplift >= 0.05, median Spearman uplift >= 0.05, at least 2 positive seeds.
 - E3: median relative gap reduction >= 0.30 or median normalized abs gap <= 0.05.
3. Diagnostics (non-decision):
 - Scale context (absolute and normalized gap statistics).
 - Oracle gap context (>0.05 and >0.10 sensitivity).
 - Seed sign consistency and variance.
